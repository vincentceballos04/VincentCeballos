Momentum

Concept, Mobile, Prototype

Momentum is a mobile fitness tracking app concept I designed to help users build sustainable workout habits through intuitive progress tracking and motivational features. The project explored creating an engaging mobile experience that balances data visualization with clean, minimalist design principles to keep users focused on their fitness goals.
