Lantern & Latte

Concept, Website, Visualisation, Prototype

Lantern & Latte is a concept cafe website that I created that combines cozy aesthetics with modern web design. The project focused on creating an inviting digital experience that reflects the warm, welcoming atmosphere of a neighborhood coffee shop, with responsive layouts and intuitive navigation for browsing menus and learning about the space.
